# 🌊 TsunamiSense AI

### AI-Powered Tsunami Risk Assessment & Decision-Support Platform

> **Understand the risk before the wave arrives.**

TsunamiSense AI is an AI-powered research and decision-support platform designed to analyze earthquake events and estimate their potential tsunami-generation risk using machine learning, geographic context, historical data, and explainable AI.

The platform combines an interactive geographic map, earthquake parameters, a machine-learning prediction pipeline, structured risk assessment, and Gemini-powered explanations into a single workflow.

> ⚠️ **Disclaimer:** TsunamiSense AI is a research and decision-support prototype. It is **not an official tsunami warning, forecasting, or emergency alert system** and must not be used as a replacement for authoritative tsunami-warning agencies.

---

## ✨ Overview

TsunamiSense AI transforms earthquake information into an understandable risk assessment.

A user can:

- Select an earthquake location directly on an interactive map
- Extract precise latitude and longitude
- Enter earthquake magnitude and focal depth
- Run the machine-learning risk assessment
- View the resulting risk score and risk level
- Understand the contributing factors
- Receive a natural-language explanation through Gemini
